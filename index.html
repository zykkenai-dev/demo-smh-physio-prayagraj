What we treat

From a stiff neck to walking again after surgery.

Every plan starts with an assessment. Treatment combines hands-on therapy, exercise and the right machine for the job.

01

Shoulder & neck

Frozen shoulder, cervical pain, stiffness from desk work or sleeping wrong.

02

Knee & joints

Arthritis, knee pain in older adults, sports strains and ligament injuries.

03

Back & spine

Lower-back pain, sciatica and slip-disc care, with posture correction.

04

After surgery

Knee and hip replacement, fractures and orthopaedic surgery rehab.

05

Neuro rehab

Stroke recovery, facial palsy and balance work, paced for each patient.

Jaw (TMJ) pain

Clicking, locking or pain in the jaw joint, treated gently.

Your first visit

Three steps. No guesswork.

Bring any X-rays, reports or prescriptions you have. Comfortable clothes help.

1

Assess

We find where the pain comes from, check movement and strength, and look at your reports.

2

Treat

Same-day relief where possible with manual therapy and equipment, then a plan of sessions.

3

Recover

Simple home exercises and regular progress checks, so the pain doesn't come back.
